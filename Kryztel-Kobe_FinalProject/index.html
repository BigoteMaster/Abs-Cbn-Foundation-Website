How to Donate

Nurture the future and support children's rights and development

Started in 1997, Bantay Bata 163 caters to child protection and family development. Its flagship programs include Helpline 163 – the pioneer hotline to report child-related cases. Bantay Bata 163 has extended its services to include interventions that cater to mental health and help schools and communities be safer spaces for children.

Impact of your donation:
Children's Rights:

Your Php 250 goes a long way in making children's rights training and awareness materials for the youth, schools, and families.

Education:

Your Php 1000 can help change lives through the continued education of Senior High School and Alternative Learning System students in underserved areas.

Disaster Resilience:

Your Php 1000 can also bring timely help where it's needed by providing 1 relief pack + 1 hygiene kit to families in times of disaster.

Environment:

Your Php 2000 makes an impact by supporting the training of Citizen Scientists who protect coral reefs, seagrass, and mangrove ecosystems.

Sustainable Livelihood:

Your Php 2500 empowers underserved communities through sustainable livelihood projects.

About ABS-CBN Foundation

ABS-CBN Foundation, Inc. is committed to serving the Filipino people through child protection (Bantay Bata 163), disaster resilience (Sagip Kapamilya), environmental conservation (Bantay Kalikasan), education, and sustainable livelihood programs across the Philippines.
